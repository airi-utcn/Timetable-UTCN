UTCN identity assets

These SVG files are unmodified official university assets, retrieved on 15 November 2026:

- https://utcluj.ro/images/logos/utcn-logo-color.svg
- https://utcluj.ro/images/logos/utcn-logo-light.svg

Reference: https://utcluj.ro/index.php/institutie-transparenta/identitate-vizuala

The linked identity manual (pages 5–18) documents the logo variants and print colors. Its primary print red is C5 M100 Y100 K20; the official digital color SVG supplies #A9261E and #231F20. Interface accent colors follow that digital reference, with adjusted shades for contrast. The light SVG is used on charcoal surfaces. Logos retain their original viewBox, proportions and colors, with surrounding whitespace; do not apply CSS filters or substitute a drawn approximation.
